Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 10, 2026

GeezAnebabeb does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use the App.

The App does not collect or store:

  • Names
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Dates of birth
  • Location information
  • Children's personal information
  • User accounts or login credentials
  • Health or medical information
  • Payment information

Data Collection and Sharing

GeezAnebabeb does not use advertising services, analytics services, or third-party data collection services.

We do not sell, rent, or share users' personal information with third parties.

Children's Privacy

GeezAnebabeb does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Because the App does not require an account or collect personal information, users can use the learning features without providing personal information.

Data Security

Because GeezAnebabeb does not collect or store personal information, there is no personal user data stored by the App that requires protection or deletion.
